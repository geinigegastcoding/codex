# YouTube policy production checklist

Checked against official YouTube guidance on 3 August 2026:

- Channel monetization policies: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Altered/synthetic content disclosure: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Monetizable content rights: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2490020
- Impersonation policy: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801947

## Inauthentic or mass-produced content

**Policy meaning:** repetitive, mass-produced or template-driven videos with little meaningful variation can be ineligible for monetization.

**Production check:** every brief must contain an original test, explanation, dataset, decision or demonstration. Swapping tool names in the same script/visual template fails QA.

## Reused material

**Policy meaning:** permission alone does not make minimally transformed material monetizable; substantial original commentary and value are required, and copyright applies separately.

**Production check:** competitor frames are private research only. Published footage must be owned/licensed and materially support original explanation. Record rights in `sources.md` or asset notes.

## Synthetic content

**Policy meaning:** realistic content that makes a real person appear to say or do something they did not can require disclosure. Cloning one’s own voice is generally listed as a minor edit; a third-party cloned voice is not covered by that exemption.

**Production check:** if a third-party cloned voice is used, document permission, set the metadata disclosure decision, and select the altered/synthetic-content disclosure during upload when required. Disclosure does not itself reduce monetization eligibility according to YouTube guidance.

## Identity and endorsement

**Production check:** do not copy channel branding, recognizable visual presentation, name or voice in a way that implies affiliation. Never imply the voice owner created, owns or endorses the channel.

## Rights

**Production check:** verify commercial rights for every visual, font, sound effect, music track and recording. No copyrighted music is bundled.
