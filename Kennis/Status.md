Current snapshot of the Magisdata project.

The primary focus is building and optimizing the public Magisdata website (`magisdata.nl`) to establish authority, target local SEO in the Leiden region for small businesses (specifically plumbers and barbers), and acquire the first customer.

6. **Marketing**: Begin Leiden-region outreach (targeting local plumbers, barbers, and similar small businesses) and prepare Instagram/social marketing setup.
7. **Dashboard**: Brainstorm dashboard features and map user flows in Google Flow.
