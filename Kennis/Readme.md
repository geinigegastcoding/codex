# Readme

Welcome to the Magisdata Knowledge Base (Kennis). This Obsidian vault stores the memory, context, and strategic direction of Magisdata.

## What it is
Magisdata is a Dutch website and SEO partner for small local service businesses (such as plumbers and barbers), offering AI-ready structures (SEO, AEO, GEO, and AI-vindbaarheid) and conversion-focused websites. The vault is used to maintain project context, founder goals, and strategic decisions.

## Why it matters
As a sole proprietorship, Magisdata is closely tied to its founder, [[Daniel Magis]]. This vault ensures that both the founder and AI assistants have a single source of truth for the business's identity, strategic direction, and daily progress.

## Who it is for
- **[[Daniel Magis]]** (Founder / The Brain)
- **AI Assistants** (Executor / Coding and Implementation)

## How AI should help
- **Cooperation Model**: Respect the division of labor. Daniel is the strategic brain; the AI is the technical executor responsible for coding, implementation, and maintaining quality.
- **Ponytail Philosophy**: Focus on YAGNI, standard/native platform features, and minimal, clean code. Keep it lightweight and fast.
- **High-Trust Branding**: Prioritize Leiden-region commercial intent and high-quality, honest content. Avoid over-engineering, unproven claims, fake reviews, or glowing design elements.
- **Automation**: Help automate logging and status tracking in [[progress]] and [[Status]].
