# MagisData

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Dutch entrepreneurs and small business teams with recurring email, leads, reports and knowledge work. Based on the current website and the user's September 7, 2026 brief.

## Product Purpose
Help businesses use AI and automation practically, with understandable systems and human control. The homepage must explain the offer, audience, problems, reason to trust, and next action.

## Capabilities and Constraints
AI audit, implementation, and enablement. Existing Next.js static export, contact form through Formspree, savings calculator, consent-aware event tracking, SEO routes and schema. No approved prices or verified customer results. Preserve existing integrations and routes. Local development branch only, no production deployment.

## Brand Commitments
MagisData remains the company name. The user explicitly replaces the old design, typography, layout and palette with a distinctive new professional identity. Original generated imagery is required. No mockups, design companions, fake proof, fake dashboards or generic SaaS template. Creative decisions are delegated to the agent.

## Evidence on Hand
Website source, company website.md, Business_Data.md, Products and Pricing.md and Daniel Magis.md. Workflow examples are illustrative; no client results or logos may be invented.

## Product Principles
Clear offer and concrete next step. Human review where needed. Understandable implementation and handover. Scope and price agreed after understanding the process.
