# Ollo recording shot list

Capture bank for [[GLOBAL-UGC-SCRIPTS]]. This is a practical shot plan, not a request to record real personal usage. The owner approved English UI18October. Use the separately verified English-first review candidate for new takes; [[ENGLISH-UI-PREPARATION]] records build identity, verification and remaining limits. If using Dutch UI, preserve every real label and include a visible **APP SHOWN IN DUTCH** cue in the final cut; never cover controls with translated text or imply that the app itself is in English. Record build identity and UI language in the take log. The owner's installed app remains untouched.

## Before recording

- **Current launch preview:**0.3.0 adds editable installed-app suggestions and a non-gating Pro preview. Every current feature stays free. Keep the Test Store price, simulated checkout and generic hosted RevenueCat paywall out of launch-value footage: they do not show the final product price, finished Pro extras or Ollo branding. Retain truthful purchase-service/privacy copy; do not claim the whole app has no internet permission. The normal free app flow is the recording subject until the production offer is configured.

- Use a separate, owner-approved review device/build or explicitly disposable test setup. Do not uninstall, overwrite or clear the user's installed test app for this session. Keep the actual version/build identity in the session notes; this is not proof of public release.
- Prefer the English review candidate. For older Dutch-build footage, use a visible language cue in any public cut; English captions explain the video and must not look like app controls.
- Use a clean demo setup with no personal accounts, contact names, messages, app history, private notifications or identifying wallpaper. Turn off notification previews and clear the notification shade before each take. Prefer a neutral test app/package; do not display real social feeds or personal app inventory.
- Make a separate seeded fixture only for stats. It must contain synthetic data and keep a conspicuous **FICTIONAL DEMO DATA** marker over every stats frame, even in close-ups or transitions. Never film a person's actual usage history, and never remove the in-app demo/coverage labels.
- Record vertical, steady, readable footage at native UI scale; capture taps with a short hold before and after each action so an editor can cut without guessing. Let every decision screen sit long enough to read the prompt, state and result. Do not accelerate a timer or skip a quiz answer in post.
- Keep a text log of shot ID, build/version, UI language, setup state, take number and any issue. Do not include package lists, exported app data, account names or device identifiers in that log.

## Optional new-feature take — R09

Use only on a separate compatible review device that already has the purpose-cue feature from local source `b64c189`. The owner's currently installed APK may not contain it; do not uninstall or replace that installation to get this shot. R01–R08 remain the core bank.

- **R09-A:** after the ordinary prompt or a legitimately completed quiz, open **Choose a purpose and time**. Hold the purpose choices, select one and five minutes, then press **Open with this purpose**. Keep the real labels; include the Dutch-build cue only if the recorded UI is Dutch.
- **R09-B:** keep that app in front for five actual minutes without turning the screen off. Set the existing session allowance long enough beforehand. Record the actual **Time for a check-in** screen and all three actions, with a two-second readable hold. Preserve the raw take and note the real elapsed gap for the **5 MINUTES LATER** edit.
- **R09-C:** record **Done, go Home** returning Home. Capture continue/dismiss separately only if needed; do not splice them into one impossible sequence.
- If another time rule intervenes, film the truthful block or retake with a suitable test setup. Never bypass a rule, shorten the cue through debug tools, repaint controls or imply Ollo detects task completion. No personal feeds or activity outcomes.

## Optional local-storage take — R10

For Script8, use a clean compatible review setup already running the verified Privacy/export wording. This does not require reinstalling the owner's test app, exporting data or recording usage statistics.

- **R10-A:** from Settings, open **Privacy & system access**. Hold the actual paragraph beginning **Ollo stores your app rules** long enough to read its local-storage/no-account explanation; record entry and a steady three-second hold. Include **APP SHOWN IN DUTCH** only for Dutch-build footage.
- **R10-B:** return to Settings and capture the export explanation beginning **You choose where to save an export**, including its cloud-provider boundary. Hold three seconds. Do not tap CSV/JSON or open a provider, and do not film any personal export payload.
- Record build identity and the two shot IDs in the take log. These shots show real product copy; they do not independently prove networking behavior. Use the source-checked script claim boundaries, with no fabricated lock, security badge or account screen added to the app footage.

## Take acceptance

Keep a take only if the action is real, the intended screen state is legible, no personal data/notifications appear, and the recording language matches the planned market cut. Retake a missed tap or obscured label; never patch the app screen by repainting, replacing, or generating text. For stats, reject any frame where the fictional-data marker is absent or unreadable. For essential/emergency routes, reject any take that has not been tested on the captured device.

The scripts are currently preproduction drafts. No recording has been supplied, English UI is approved, no public destination is known, and no ad has been generated, submitted, published or tested with viewers.
