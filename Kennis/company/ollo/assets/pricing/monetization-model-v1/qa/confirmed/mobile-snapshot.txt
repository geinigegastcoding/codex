How the arithmetic works

For each one-time purchase: tax-exclusive price = gross price ÷ (1 + VAT rate), rounded to cents; modeled VAT = gross price − rounded tax-exclusive price; modeled Play fee = chosen fee base × fee rate, rounded to cents; proceeds before refunds = gross − modeled VAT − modeled fee.

Refunds are an explicit simplifying assumption: the chosen refund share reverses that net proceeds amount proportionally. Support cost is then subtracted for every new buyer, including refunded buyers. Monthly modeled contribution = per-purchase contribution × new purchases − fixed monthly costs. Break-even purchases = ceiling(fixed monthly costs ÷ positive per-purchase contribution); if contribution is zero or negative, no break-even count is shown. Zero monthly volume still leaves fixed monthly costs.

Rounding is per line to the nearest cent; the fee is computed from the selected base before rounding. This is not intended to reproduce Play's settlement ledger.
