# Creative brief: quiz opening concept video

**Status:** unpublished local concept. **Format:** silent 9:16, 10 seconds, 1080×1920.

The video shows the moment before someone opens a chosen app, then makes Ollo’s optional three-question quiz feel small and finite. It uses the existing Ollo mascot and quiz illustration, warm off-white paper, orange sparks, charcoal typography, and restrained motion. No simulated app screen, feed, download/store CTA, music, testimonial, outcome promise, or destination is included. A small “CONCEPT” label stays visible.

| Time | Copy | Picture / motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.6s | “Een app openen?” | The actual orange pixel mascot bobs beneath the line. |
| 1.4–6.0s | “OPTIONEEL” / “Eerst drie vragen.” / “vóór je een gekozen app opent.” | The existing text-free quiz illustration settles in with a gentle scale-up; the mascot sprite fades away as the illustration mascot takes over. Sparks peak at 3.25, 4.04 and 4.82 seconds. |
| 4.35–7.22s | “Schaken · Rekenen · Wist je dat?” | The topic line appears below the illustration, then fades as the final title arrives. |
| 6.1–10.0s | “Drie vragen. Jij kiest.” / “Een optionele quiz vóór je een gekozen app opent.” | The final title holds over the illustration; no action implies a public destination. |

The wording describes the current optional opening quiz and selected-app scope. The illustration is a metaphor rather than an app screenshot. The ad does not promise improved learning, reduced phone use, exclusive features, Play availability, or measured results.

**Editable source:** `render.mjs`; **render record:** `verification.json`; **poster:** `poster.png`. The script uses the desktop’s already-bundled Sharp and installed FFmpeg only; no Remotion, downloads, new packages, or external media.
