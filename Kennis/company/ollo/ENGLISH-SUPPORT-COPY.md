# English answers for the global launch

Source-backed draft updated18October for the English0.3.0-preview launch candidate, including RevenueCat Test Store integration. Older English0.2.0 and Dutch review APKs may behave differently; record the actual build before answering. They have not been published or sent to anyone. English UI was explicitly approved; [[ENGLISH-UI-PREPARATION]] records verification and remaining limits. Existing Dutch installations retain their own labels until a compatible update is chosen.

- **Do I need an account?** Ollo does not require an app account. Your usage history, rules and selected apps stay on your device. The RevenueCat purchase integration uses an anonymous purchase identity and network traffic for purchase options/customer info, including when Ollo starts. No ad network, PostHog or Firebase service is configured. See the purchase/privacy explanation for that separate data flow.
- **Do I need to pay during testing?** No. Every current feature stays free. **Settings → Ollo Pro** is a launch preview; themes, extra learning packs and custom presets are still ideas, not finished paid features.
- **Why does Ultimate show a test price?** This debug build reads a sandbox catalog. **Test Store** purchases are simulations with no real charge; the displayed test value is not the planned launch price. Production pricing and the branded hosted paywall will be configured later.
- **What does Restore purchase restore?** It checks RevenueCat for active Ollo Pro purchase access. It does not restore deleted app settings, usage history or JSON exports. A Test Store result does not prove a Google Play purchase; that requires separate launch testing.
- **Why does Ollo ask for Android access?** Usage access supplies Android's available app-use history. Accessibility access detects which app is in front and displays your selected rules. Ollo is configured without screen-content retrieval. Android controls both permissions.
- **Why does Android warn about “full control”?** Android may show a broad Accessibility warning. Ollo's current service uses app/window changes to apply your rules, show prompts and return Home when you choose. It is configured without screen-content retrieval and does not read messages or record your screen. You can decline or turn this access off in Android; app blocking then stops. Usage figures use separate Usage Access.
- **Why does protection say it is disconnected?** Open **Settings → App protection** and check Ollo's Accessibility access. Usage figures have a separate **Usage access** setting. Device behavior varies; restarting Ollo is not a guaranteed fix.
- **Are quizzes required?** They are optional and off by default. Under **Learn before opening**, choose chess, math or facts. When enabled for an opening prompt, answer three questions correctly before choosing to open. A quiz cannot lift a fixed block.
- **What does “choose a purpose” do?** At a decision prompt or completed quiz, **Choose a purpose and time** lets you choose a reason and 5, 10 or 20 minutes. The check-in appears only while that app is in front. You can finish and go Home, restart the cue, or continue without it. Ollo cannot detect whether your task is done; your existing time rules still apply. This temporary cue clears when the screen turns off, protection disconnects/restarts, access expires or a hard block takes over.
- **Can I still reach something I need?** Essentials and phone/alarm routes are available. **I need an app** offers one selected app a two-minute exception while the underlying pause continues. Check these routes on your own device. For urgent help, use the device's normal emergency controls.
- **Does a dash in my chart mean zero?** No. **Not yet known** or **—** means the value is unknown. Available Android history and Ollo's measurement since active protection can be incomplete; the coverage notes explain the difference.
- **Can I save or restore my data?** Settings can save CSV reports and a JSON file through Android's document picker. Wait for the saved confirmation. If saving fails, a partial file may remain; retry in a destination you control. These exports cannot currently restore Ollo. A cloud destination is managed by its provider.
- **What does “delete measurements” remove?** It clears Ollo's local usage intervals, baseline and day outcomes. Your rules and morning-start records remain. It cannot delete Android's history, exported files or RevenueCat purchase records. Purchase-data requests need the provider support/deletion process documented before launch.
- **The update will not install. Should I uninstall?** Keep your installation and data. The current review build uses a development signing key, which may differ from your installed version. Ask the release owner to check the certificates before changing anything. JSON exports are not restore files.
- **What should I send for support?** Your Ollo/Android version, device model, expected and observed behavior, and short reproduction steps. Redact visible names. Do not send usage exports, databases, app inventories, private screens or notifications.
