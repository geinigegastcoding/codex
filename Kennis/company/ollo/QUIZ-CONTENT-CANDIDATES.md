## Source and scope notes

That candidate was excluded rather than accepted from the search extract. An official USFS leaf-color PDF also failed to open, so it was not used. The six cards above rely on five successfully opened primary pages; the cephalopod page supports two cards. The bee source is a named researcher's conference abstract hosted by USDA, not a USDA-authored classroom guide. No unstable record, ranking, or medical/legal/financial claim is included.

Parent independently opened the cephalopod, bee, NPS, butterfly and NOAA sources. Copy was simplified, “waggeldans” corrected, an unnecessary dance-duration claim omitted, and answer positions balanced across 0/1/2. A structural check passed for all six unique IDs, three distinct choices each, intended answer/index agreement and two answers at each position. This is editorial/content verification, not user comprehension or curriculum validation.
