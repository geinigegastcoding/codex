## Product

Ollo helps someone pause automatic app opening and choose their next action. The Android app combines selected-app blocking, intentional opening, short sessions, routines, optional three-question offline chess/math/fact exercises, alternatives and conservative usage statistics. The0.3.0preview source is Android10+, Kotlin/Compose and English-first. It has no Ollo account, ad network, PostHog or Firebase. Rules/usage remain local; RevenueCat10.28.1 adds purchase/customer-info/paywall service traffic. Dashboard and native billing acceptance remain pending.

Every current feature stays free during testing. Ollo Pro is a non-gating test preview; special future extras/customization and around€15lifetime access are proposals, with no finished paid catalog or real production sale. It is voluntary self-control: Android settings and uninstall remain accessible. Essentials and app-scoped emergency routes exist; physical-device acceptance remains necessary. Selective Shorts/Reels, website filtering, widgets, background notification reminders and cross-device recovery are proposals.

Local source `b64c189`, checked18October, now adds an optional preset purpose and5/10/20minute foreground check-in; default5. Finish/Home, continue and dismiss remain voluntary, while existing time rules still apply. The plan is temporary, not task-completion detection or a notification service. The catalog also now has twelve fact cards. These refinements are locally verified source candidates and are not implied to exist in the owner's installation or the earlier uploaded review APK. ## Audience and job hypothesis

The current market direction is global and English-first. A starting audience hypothesis remains Android users who notice automatic openings of selected apps and want a small, voluntary pause while retaining practical phone functions. The first wedge is the moment before opening a chosen app, not a clinical population or “screen-time cure.” This has not been validated in any country or language. Jobs to test: interrupt an automatic opening; set a chosen time window; understand personal usage with clear measurement limits. No B2B persona or company-size model applies.

## Problem and alternatives

The working problem is the gap between wanting to check one useful thing and opening an app out of habit. Alternatives include Android Digital Wellbeing, existing blockers (one sec, AppBlock, Opal), removing apps and personal routines. Competitor feature/price claims need current platform-specific evidence; see [[PRICING]] and [[FEATURE-RESEARCH]]. Their existence alone does not prove user dissatisfaction.

## Differentiation hypothesis

A memorable orange pixel companion, finite optional offline learning, intentional opening and transparent local measurement form a coherent identity. These are differentiating ingredients, not evidence that no competitor offers them. The strongest candidate promise is a small deliberate pause with a clear finish. Do not promise addiction treatment, saved time, improved learning, complete historical coverage or unbreakable protection.

The 17 October primary-source Android overlap check in [[FEATURE-RESEARCH]] found direct learning-gate alternatives. Keep the combined experience as a positioning hypothesis; neither a quiz, adaptive review, local data nor mascot color is an exclusive feature. The current content bank is small, so no paid-learning catalog or outcome promise is established. Global reach, English-language fit and channel performance remain hypotheses; no representative market research or demand evidence is in the supplied context.

## Objections and switching

Likely questions to validate: why Accessibility access is necessary; whether essentials will remain reachable; whether quizzes become annoying; whether another app can help; and whether data stays local. Respond with actual disclosure, a practice block, selectable rules, optional quizzes and current privacy facts. Unverified compatibility must remain explicit. Someone seeking parental/device-owner control, cross-platform sync or a guaranteed feed-only block is outside the current offer.

## Language and voice

The user explicitly asks for a pixelated orange mascot with spark and an interface that is beautifully simple. These are founder design instructions, not customer quotes. New marketing copy is English-first for a global market; an English-first review candidate is prepared. Use warm, direct language. The mascot is playful and energetic; functional controls stay native and readable. Avoid guilt, detox language, fake urgency and punitive streaks. Earlier Dutch-first campaign lines and concepts remain drafts, not validated copy.

## Proof and goals
