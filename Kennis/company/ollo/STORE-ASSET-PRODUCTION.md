# Ollo Google Play screenshot production brief

Produce six ordered English-language phone screenshots from the real stable 0.3.0 app (`c2adee7f`, source `4db5eb2`) on a clean review setup. These are capture instructions, not generated assets. Keep original, unaltered captures and record build, device/emulator, resolution, UI language and setup in a take log. The product UI is orange pixel-styled; frame the real UI cleanly and use restrained orange/cream/charcoal framing only if it does not obscure or alter app content.

## Ordered six-screen set

1. **Choose your apps** — Capture onboarding’s installed-app suggestions with a small, neutral test fixture. Show one distraction and one essential selected and the editable states. Avoid exposing a full personal app list. Do not imply Ollo inspects app content.
2. **Set a routine** — Show Rules with one clearly named demo schedule and its actual saved time/days/state. This is staged configuration, not a user result. Make the rule type legible; never suggest a prompt schedule defeats a fixed block.
3. **A pause with a choice** — Capture the actual opening prompt for a neutral test app, at the ready state, with the genuine controls visible. Do not show a quiz, hide a countdown or imply the prompt appears before all possible app/audio transitions. No reliability guarantee in overlay text.
4. **Browse offline ideas** — Capture the actual Ideas screen with a real time/energy filter and one real card. Show browsing only; do not imply an activity was completed or that Ollo tracked it.
5. **See what Android reports** — Use a separate seeded synthetic stats fixture. Show a day with partial/unknown coverage and retain the app’s coverage labels. Add a clear, high-contrast **FICTIONAL DEMO DATA** label to the asset, persistent and readable at phone-listing scale. Never use actual personal history, fabricate numbers in post, or imply complete coverage.
6. **Keep the essentials close** — Capture the real Essentials screen or selection state on the reviewed device, with neutral test entries. Show the actual screen labels and selection state. This describes configuration only; do not claim universal access, successful calls/alarms across OEMs, or uncircumventable enforcement. If the real screen cannot fit cleanly at native scale, use Settings showing actual CSV/JSON export choices as the truthful alternative and make no claim that an export occurred.

Do not use the remembered-opening-choice UI: it is source-only and awaits native acceptance. Omit RevenueCat/Test Store checkout, price, Pro preview, a hosted paywall, purchase success, and any unfinished/unconfigured production offer. Do not use a staged screenshot as if it proves runtime reliability. The reported startup quiz/audio leak, shade bypass, hardware-key dismissal and spurious retriggers are unresolved; screenshots must not imply those transition cases are fixed.

## Capture and delivery requirements

- Capture genuine app UI from the exact reviewed build. No drawn, generated, repainted, translated-over, or composite app screen. Keep real app labels intact. A small localized caption outside the UI may clarify a feature; avoid clutter and keep any text overlay brief and readable.
- Prepare phone portrait captures at **1080 × 1920 px (9:16)** where the device/build permits. Google Play’s current general screenshot minimum is 320 px, maximum 3840 px, with max dimension no more than twice the min; JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha. Its recommendation guidance calls for at least four 1080 px screenshots for recommendation surfaces; actual app experience should dominate the first three. Up to eight are allowed per device type.
- Remove excess status-bar notifications and service-provider clutter. Use a clean test device/profile, neutral wallpaper, no personal packages, feeds, contacts, exported files or identifying account data. Do not place device mockups, store badges, ratings, awards, pricing or calls to action around the screenshots.
- Provide alt text for all six assets, each 140 characters or fewer. Proposed descriptions:
  1. “Ollo onboarding shows editable choices for distraction apps and essentials.”
  2. “Ollo Rules shows a configured demo schedule and its saved state.”
  3. “Ollo displays an opening prompt with the available choice controls.”
  4. “Ollo Ideas shows an activity card with time and energy browsing.”
  5. “Ollo usage view labels partial coverage; displayed values are fictional demo data.”
  6. “Ollo Essentials shows configured neutral test apps kept within selected access routes.”
- Review each asset at actual phone listing scale. Reject unreadable labels, accidental personal data, stale build UI, implied outcomes, missing fictional-data label, or any state not reproduced in the raw capture. Save the raw originals and note any crop or external caption in the take log.

## Official source reviewed 18 October 2026

[Google Play Console Help: Add preview assets to showcase your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en-en) — screenshot requirements and recommendations: minimum two across different device types to publish; JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha; general 320–3840 px bounds and 2:1 maximum dimension ratio; up to eight per device type; recommendation eligibility calls for four 1080 px captures and actual app experience; brief overlays and alt text up to 140 characters. The page also says a feature graphic is required to publish: JPEG/24-bit PNG without alpha, 1024 × 500 px. This brief does not create or approve that separate asset.
