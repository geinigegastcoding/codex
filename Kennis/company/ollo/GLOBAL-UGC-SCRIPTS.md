# Ollo global UGC script drafts

Eight English-first, 15–30 second demonstration concepts for future short-form ads. The owner approved English UI18October. Prefer the separately verified English-first review candidate for new recordings; [[ENGLISH-UI-PREPARATION]] gives its build and verification scope. If Dutch footage is used, preserve the actual controls and label it visibly **APP SHOWN IN DUTCH**. Never cover Dutch controls with English labels that imply the app itself is translated. The owner's installed app was not replaced. Script7 requires the newer purpose-cue build and can be omitted from the first recording session. Script8 adds an optional Privacy take without requiring an export or personal history.

Current product facts were checked against `app/src/main/java/app/ollo/ui/Onboarding.kt`, `OlloApp.kt`, `app/src/main/java/app/ollo/platform/OlloAccessibilityService.kt`, and `app/src/main/java/app/ollo/domain/OpeningQuiz.kt` on 18 October 2026. The review build is 0.2.0-review; there is no public store destination or paid offer established. The target audience, global demand and all creative performance are unvalidated hypotheses. See [[product-marketing-context]] for the current positioning status.

**Positioning check,18October:** [Stop Me](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.khueon.stopme) advertises purpose/session decisions; [LLLock](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_ZA&id=com.lllock.app) advertises a learning gate. These are developer listings, not independent product tests. Demonstrate Ollo's specific finite chess/math/facts choices, voluntary check-in and pixel character; make no first/only/unique or learning-outcome claim. Exact feature and timing gaps are in [[FEATURE-RESEARCH]].

## Production rules

- AI scenes are visual hooks for an explainer, not testimonials. Label AI actor footage **AI-GENERATED VISUAL · PRODUCT DEMO**; invent no user story or outcome.
- Time ranges are seconds from clip start. AI lines are planned VO/captions over an actor or B-roll shot; `R##` means genuine app footage from [[RECORDING-SHOT-LIST]].
- Flow prompts are standalone visual drafts: no generated Ollo UI, words or logos. They assume no audio, continuity or editing feature; use a human-made equivalent if needed.
- Keep actual app labels intact. No store badge, price, public availability or unverified destination.
- Stats footage must carry **FICTIONAL DEMO DATA** on every frame. No real history, app inventory, notifications or account data.

|---|---|---|

| 18–24s | `REAL APP RECORDING R09-C`: press the actual finish/Home action, then end on approved mascot artwork. | VO: “You decide when you're done. Ollo doesn't detect it for you.” Card: **Ollo · Open with a reason.** |
