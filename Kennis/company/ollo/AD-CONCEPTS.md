# Ollo short-form ad concepts

> Historical Dutch concepts. Current marketing is English/global: use [[GLOBAL-UGC-SCRIPTS]] and [[RECORDING-SHOT-LIST]]. The old no-internet-permission claim below is obsolete after RevenueCat integration; do not use it in new recordings or ads.

Prepared 16 October 2026. These scripts are **creative hypotheses** for organic validation and future launch use. They are not claims about measured outcomes. Use only after the intended release is publicly installable; private review footage is for invited reviewers and must be labeled as such.

## Shared production direction

Shoot 9:16 phone footage at 1080×1920 (or higher), with the real current Ollo screen and large Dutch burned-in captions. Keep the pixel orange mascot crisp, expressive and energetic: little spark bursts, hops, sign flips, pixel confetti. Palette: Ollo orange, warm white, charcoal. Avoid default corporate stock footage and “sad person in the dark” tropes. Audio can be voiceover plus a short original chiptune cue; captions must carry the story with sound off. Leave top/bottom UI-safe margins.

## 1. “Eén bericht” — familiar moment, warm humor

**Length:** 18 seconds  
**Hook (0–2s, on-screen):** “Ik open Instagram voor één bericht.”  
**Shots:** 0–3s: phone in hand; pixel mascot pops in with a tiny envelope. 3–6s: real Ollo opening prompt appears. 6–10s: thumb pauses; tap “Open bewust”. 10–14s: show Today screen and chosen-app rule. 14–18s: mascot delivers envelope and points to CTA.  
**VO:** “Eén bericht. En dan… waar was ik ook alweer? Ollo zet een klein beslismoment voor apps die jij kiest. Jij bepaalt daarna zelf.”  
**End card:** “Even pauze. Jij kiest. Bekijk Ollo.”  
**CTA:** “Bekijk hoe Ollo werkt.”  
**English variant:** Hook: “I opened Instagram for one message.” VO: “One message. Then—wait, what was I doing? Ollo adds a small pause to apps you choose. You decide what happens next.” End: “A small pause. Your choice.”  
**Claim note:** Don’t stage a fake user testimonial. This is a dramatized scenario; label it “situatie nagespeeld” if people might mistake it for real footage.

## 2. “Kies je level” — quiz as optional playful twist

**Length:** 20 seconds  
**Hook:** Mascot flips a pixel sign: “Nog vóór je opent: 3 vragen?”  
**Shots:** 0–2s sign flip. 2–6s Settings shows “Leer vóór openen” and quiz topics. 6–12s show real chess or math question and answer feedback. 12–16s show completion then the separate “Open bewust” action. 16–20s mascot celebrates with a tiny pixel spark.  
**VO:** “Zet de optionele quiz aan voor apps die jij kiest. Drie goede antwoorden, dan kies je bewust of je opent.”  
**End card:** “Schaken, rekenen of wist-je-dat. Optioneel in Ollo.”  
**CTA:** “Ontdek de kleine challenge.”  
**English variant:** “Turn on an optional quiz for the apps you choose. Get three right, then make a conscious choice to open.” End: “Chess, math or a quick fact. Optional in Ollo.”  
**Claim note:** The quiz is off by default. It does not override a fixed block, daily limit or protected pause. Make “optional” readable on-screen.

## 3. “Je essentials blijven” — safety and control

**Length:** 17 seconds  
**Hook:** “Een pauze-app die je telefoon niet onbruikbaar maakt.”  
**Shots:** 0–2s mascot holds a “PAUZE” sign. 2–7s app selection shows distraction choices beside essentials. 7–11s show calls/alarms/essentials route in actual UI. 11–14s show user-selected block. 14–17s mascot steps aside and opens a clear path.  
**VO:** “Kies zelf welke apps Ollo onderbreekt. Je essentials, telefoon en alarm blijven belangrijk. Dit is jouw vrijwillige pauze.”  
**End card:** “Meer keuze. Minder autopiloot.”  
**CTA:** “Bekijk de instellingen.”  
**English variant:** “Choose which apps Ollo pauses. Essentials, calls and alarms still matter. Ollo is a voluntary pause you control.”  
**Claim note:** Don’t guarantee every OEM/device configuration. Use wording “designed routes” or show only tested device footage until physical-device acceptance is complete.

## 4. “Geen account. Geen feed.” — privacy by architecture

**Length:** 15 seconds  
**Hook:** “Een app over je apps, zonder account?”  
**Shots:** 0–2s mascot stamps “GEEN ACCOUNT”. 2–6s simple pixel animation of Ollo storing its own settings on-device, with a separate user-chosen export path. 6–10s actual privacy screen: local-only explanation; no internet permission called out. 10–13s show chosen-app pause. 13–15s brand lockup.  
**VO:** “Ollo bewaart instellingen en metingen lokaal. Geen account, geen server en geen internettoegang in deze build. Android-toegang moet je zelf inschakelen.”  
**End card:** “Lokaal. Vrijwillig. Van jou.”  
**CTA:** “Lees hoe Ollo met data omgaat.”  
**English variant:** “Ollo stores settings and measurements on your phone. You choose where exports go. No account, server, or internet permission in this build. Android access is still your choice.”  
**Claim note:** Use “in deze build” unless verified again against the exact release. Explain that Android Usage Access and Accessibility access are required for relevant functions; distinguish app-owned local storage from user-selected exports, which may use a cloud provider and survive deletion in Ollo. Local storage does not prevent physical access.

## 5. “De mini-pauze” — friendly mascot challenge

**Length:** 15 seconds  
**Hook:** Pixel mascot races a scrolling thumb, then plants a tiny orange stop sign.  
**Shots:** 0–3s quick playful race animation. 3–7s actual selected-app block/pause UI. 7–11s user picks resume or returns to home; show they decide. 11–15s mascot offers a “probeer 30 sec” visual only if demonstrating the existing practice flow.  
**VO:** “Je hoeft je telefoon niet weg te gooien. Soms is een klein beslismoment genoeg om zelf te kiezen.”  
**End card:** “Ollo. Even pauze voor de apps die jij kiest.”  
**CTA:** “Bekijk de demo.”  
**English variant:** “You don’t have to throw your phone away. Add a small decision point, then choose what you want to do.”  
**Claim note:** “Enough” is metaphorical, not an efficacy claim; for safer copy use “Ollo adds…” and do not imply proven behavior change.

## 6. “Jouw gebruik, zonder oordeel” — truthful stats

**Length:** 18 seconds  
**Hook:** “Een grafiek die ook durft te zeggen: gegevens ontbreken.”  
**Shots:** 0–3s mascot peers at an actual empty/coverage-labeled chart. 3–8s show real chart labels/source coverage text. 8–12s show local privacy/export controls. 12–15s mascot shrugs and offers magnifying glass. 15–18s CTA.  
**VO:** “Ollo toont beschikbare Android-gebruiksgegevens met bron en dekking. Geen verzonnen nul voor een ontbrekende dag.”  
**End card:** “Inzicht zonder oordeel.”  
**CTA:** “Bekijk hoe inzichten werken.”  
**English variant:** “Ollo labels the Android usage data that’s available, including its source and coverage. Missing data isn’t shown as zero.”  
**Claim note:** Use a real screen and current data-state label. Never fill a demo chart with invented measurements without a visible “demo” label.

## 7. “Niet nog een productiviteitspraatje” — anti-guilt positioning

**Length:** 16 seconds  
**Hook:** “Je hoeft geen betere versie van jezelf te worden.”  
**Shots:** 0–2s mascot tosses a generic “10× PRODUCTIEVER” poster offscreen. 2–7s real app shows user choosing only one app and one gentle opening prompt. 7–12s Ollo pause screen; user chooses a useful app. 12–16s mascot gives a spark, not a trophy.  
**VO:** “Ollo houdt geen score over jouw karakter bij. Je kiest een app, kiest een moment, en krijgt ruimte om zelf te beslissen.”  
**End card:** “Geen schuldgevoel. Wel een keuze.”  
**CTA:** “Ontmoet Ollo.”  
**English variant:** “You don’t need to become a better version of yourself. Choose an app, choose a moment, and give yourself room to decide.”  
**Claim note:** Avoid asserting that Ollo does not score anything if future builds add features; at present it shows conservative daily outcomes/streaks. The line “houdt geen score over jouw karakter bij” clarifies no moral judgment, not absence of all metrics.

## 8. “Wat Ollo wel en niet doet” — trust-led demo

**Length:** 22 seconds  
**Hook:** “Kan Ollo alleen Reels blokkeren? Nog niet.”  
**Shots:** 0–3s mascot holds an honest “NOG NIET” card. 3–8s show selected-app blocking working on a supported screen. 8–13s show optional opening quiz. 13–17s show “geen account / lokaal” privacy screen. 17–22s show product page with release status and supported Android details.  
**VO:** “Ollo kan apps kiezen en pauzeren, met een optionele quiz vóór openen. Alleen Reels of Shorts blokkeren staat nog niet in deze versie. Dit is wat er echt werkt.”  
**End card:** “Eerlijk over wat werkt. Ollo.”  
**CTA:** “Bekijk de actuele functies.”  
**English variant:** “Ollo can pause apps you choose and add an optional opening quiz. Selective Reels or Shorts blocking isn’t in this version. Here’s what works today.”  
**Claim note:** Best for a product page or reviewer audience; refresh the caveat if the roadmap changes. Do not buy keyword ads for selective Reels/Shorts blocking.
