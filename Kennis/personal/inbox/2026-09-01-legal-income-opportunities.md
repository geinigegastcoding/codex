### 5. Bezorgdekrant - concrete Leiden route from age 15

- Official youth page: [Jongeren bijbaan](https://www.bezorgdekrant.nl/jongeren-bijbaan/)
- Local vacancy: [Krantenwijk Leiden](https://www.bezorgdekrant.nl/vacature/krantenwijk-leiden/)
- Official recruitment page: [Start krantenbezorgen](https://start.krantenbezorgen.nl/)
- The current Leiden page says applicants can be 15 or older and displays EUR 16.19 per uurswijk. A uurswijk is a route-based fee, not a guaranteed hourly wage.
- The service says 15-17-year-olds can deliver one route; no experience is required. The Dutch government says a parent/guardian must sign the delivery agreement and sets specific limits for 15-year-olds, including rest time and morning-delivery limits. [Government rules](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/bijbaan-vakantiewerk-en-stage-door-jongeren/kranten-bezorgen-15-jaar)
- Route availability, hours, and actual payout still need confirmation. The registration form requests personal information, so do not fill it in yet.
- Gate: truthful identity, BSN/ID requirements, route safety, travel, and the parent-signed contract all need guardian review. Do not register yet.
