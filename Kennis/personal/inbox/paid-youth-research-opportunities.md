# Paid youth research opportunities

These are official university research routes, not guaranteed income. A parent/guardian should read the study information and privacy details first. Do not provide sensitive answers, contact details, or consent through a form until the study genuinely fits and the guardian agrees.

## Best conditional lead: Universiteit Utrecht Luister! youth panel

- Eligibility: ages 12-18, with experience of parents separating; the page says boys aged 8-18 and young people invited to speak with a judge or mediator are still especially sought.
- Work: approximately two-hour meet-ups in Utrecht every 2-3 months, sharing ideas about research and youth experiences.
- Reward: travel reimbursement plus a EUR 25 gift card per meet-up, with food and drinks.
- Consent: parent permission is required below age 16; participation is voluntary.
- Official pages: [youth panel](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/luister/wie-zijn-wij/jongeren-panel), [FAQ and privacy details](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/luister/info-voor-jongeren/veelgestelde-vragen), [registration route](https://survey.uu.nl/jfe/form/SV_81zLYk9rrGnIzzw).

This is only suitable if the eligibility condition is true and the travel/time commitment is realistic. The reward is a gift card, not cash.

## Higher-value but sensitive lead: STARr2.0

- Eligibility appears to include young people aged 12-20 who experience stress, anxiety, or low mood; the official training page describes the target group.
- Study burden: six longer online questionnaires over a 15-week study, plus short weekly app questionnaires.
- Reward: EUR 90 total for completed questionnaires, according to the official study page.
- Privacy/safety: the questions concern feelings, stress, anxiety, low mood, thoughts, and personal background. This is a research participation decision, not a way to chase money. A guardian should review the information and consent documents, and Daniel should participate only if comfortable.
- Official pages: [STARr2 overview](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/starr2), [questionnaire compensation](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/starr2/jongeren-en-ouders/onderzoek-achtergrond-en-opzet), [training eligibility](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/starr2/jongeren-en-ouders/starr-training).

Recruitment availability and exact consent requirements were not confirmed from the public page, so do not assume acceptance. Ask the research team through its official contact page only after guardian review.

## Lower-priority conditional lead: Minding the Gap

- Eligibility: youth panels for ages 12-25 in Liendert, Amersfoort, and Kanaleneiland, Utrecht.
- Work: occasional meetings and optional tasks such as helping with flyers, factsheets, or social-media campaigns about youth mental health and money worries.
- Reward: the official page says participants receive compensation, but it does not state the amount on the page checked.
- Contact: `mindingthegap@uu.nl` on the official project page.
- Official page: [How to participate](https://minding-the-gap.sites.uu.nl/hoe-kan-ik-meedoen/).

Treat this as a paid participation opportunity only after asking what the current compensation and time commitment are. Do not volunteer for unpaid work while assuming payment later.

## Closed lead excluded

Universiteit Leiden's online-challenges study advertised EUR 25 for two online sessions for ages 12-15, but its official notice stated an application deadline of 7 August 2026. Because the current date is 9 August 2026, it is not treated as an active lead and no late application will be sent.

## Safe activation checklist

- [ ] Guardian verifies the official university domain and current recruitment status.
- [ ] Daniel confirms the eligibility condition is truthful.
- [ ] Read the participant information, privacy statement, consent form, time commitment, and reward/payment method.
- [ ] Decide whether gift cards are acceptable; do not treat them as guaranteed cash.
- [ ] Use a guardian-approved email/contact route; do not upload ID unless the guardian has verified why it is necessary.
- [ ] Save the confirmation and study contact, but do not submit multiple duplicate forms.
