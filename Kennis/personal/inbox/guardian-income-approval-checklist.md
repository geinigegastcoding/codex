## Option B - sell existing items

- [ ] Choose only items Daniel already owns and is allowed to sell; no buying stock, counterfeits, or unclear property.
- [ ] Review the [Vinted minor rules](https://www.vinted.nl/help/1057) and [Marktplaats terms](https://www.marktplaats.nl/i/help/over-marktplaats/voorwaarden-en-privacybeleid/algemene_voorwaarden_marktplaats.pdf).
- [ ] If using Vinted, the account is in the parent/guardian's name, the adult is present, and Daniel's personal data is not uploaded.
- [ ] Guardian checks photos, condition, comparable prices, messages, payment, shipping, and any identity/tax request.
- [ ] Use a public handover location with the guardian present; never meet a stranger alone.

## Option C - neighbour services

- [ ] Choose one small daytime service: dog walking for a known neighbour, plant watering, light garden tidying, sweeping, or a basic Python explanation session.
- [ ] Confirm the job is light and safe for a 15-year-old; exclude ladders, power tools, chemicals, driving, medication, private data, and unfamiliar homes alone.
- [ ] Guardian knows the person, address, start/end time, task, price, and emergency contact before the first job.
- [ ] Agree the exact task, price, and payment before starting; record hours and income.

## Stop conditions

Stop and ask the guardian before proceeding if a site asks for an unexpected ID upload, payment, bank login, password, remote access, crypto deposit, or a contract that is not understood. Never use a false age, false identity, VPN, automation, or another person's account without the platform's explicit rules and guardian approval.
