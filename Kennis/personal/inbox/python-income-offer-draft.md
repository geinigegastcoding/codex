# Python tutoring and mini-project offer draft

This is a small, testable offer based on the existing personal Python course. It is not published and must be reviewed by a parent or guardian before any message, account, agreement, or payment.

## What can be offered

- A 60-minute beginner Python session in Dutch or English.
- One focused topic: functions, loops, lists and dictionaries, debugging, or a small word-counting project.
- A short follow-up note with the key idea, corrected example, and three next exercises.
- Optional mini-project path: word analyzer, sports league table, or route finder.

## Suggested first test

Test price (an experiment, not a market claim): EUR 15 for one 60-minute session. A parent or guardian must handle the agreement and payment. Do not promise grades, exam results, or a guaranteed outcome.

## Proof available

The local course currently contains 8 stages, 48 lessons, 24 checked exercises, and 10 guided projects. On 2026-08-09, its TypeScript check passed, all 31 automated tests passed, and the production build completed successfully. The app keeps progress in the browser and has no account or payment backend.

A separate three-challenge sample pack with hints, checks, and an answer key is drafted at `Kennis/personal/inbox/python-mini-challenge-pack-draft.md`. Its answer-key checks passed locally. It is intended as a free sample or a small guardian-managed digital product, not as an automatically published listing.

## Boundaries

- Teach and explain; do not complete graded work or impersonate a student.
- Do not request passwords, private files, API keys, or remote computer access.
- Use a guardian-approved contact and payment route.
- Keep the first session narrow; no unlimited support or open-ended revisions.
- Stop if a learner asks for cheating, unsafe scraping, or access to private systems.

## Demand test

1. Ask a guardian to choose three appropriate contacts or local groups.
2. Send the approved message manually; no bulk messaging or scraping.
3. Record replies, objections, and whether anyone asks for a paid session.
4. Only improve the offer after real feedback. Do not build a storefront first.

Proceed only if a guardian approves the offer and at least one real person asks for details. Then agree on the exact time, price, cancellation rule, and payment before accepting work.
