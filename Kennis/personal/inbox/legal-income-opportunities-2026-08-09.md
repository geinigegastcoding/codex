## Routes that do not require a company customer

- **Sell items already owned:** Vinted's current help page says a minor may use Vinted only with an adult present, with the account in the parent/guardian's name and no minor's personal data uploaded. Marktplaats' current terms allow minors with legal-representative permission or where independent action is customary. This is a low-risk way to test demand, but the guardian should control the account, payment, identity checks, messages, shipping, and handovers. [Vinted minor rules](https://www.vinted.nl/help/1057) [Marktplaats terms](https://www.marktplaats.nl/i/help/over-marktplaats/voorwaarden-en-privacybeleid/algemene_voorwaarden_marktplaats.pdf)
- **Neighbour services:** the Dutch Tax Administration gives examples including garden maintenance, babysitting, dog walking, errands, and small jobs. This is only tax context; age-15 working-time, safety, and prohibited-task rules still apply. Start with one short daytime job for a family the guardian knows. [Belastingdienst examples](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/werken/niet_in_loondienst_werken/huishoudelijke_werkzaamheden_voor_anderen/u_doet_huishoudelijk_werk_voor_iemand/werkzaamheden)
- **Python for individuals:** Preply currently has a Python tutor category and tutor-job page. Its terms allow ages 13-17 only under parent/guardian supervision and responsibility, and may request government ID and educational documents. Keep it as a backup because the privacy and platform-account burden is high; a guardian-reviewed direct local session is simpler. [Python tutors](https://preply.com/en/online/python-tutors) [Preply terms](https://termsofuse.preply.com/terms_of_use/en_TermsOfService.pdf)
- **Original digital pack on Etsy:** Etsy currently permits 13-17-year-olds to sell only through a parent/guardian-owned and supervised account. Etsy supports original digital downloads, so the locally verified Python challenge PDF is a plausible test product. Publishing still requires the guardian to review the account, fees, tax, refunds, file, and listing. [Etsy minor policy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040983333-Can-Minors-Sell-on-Etsy) [Etsy digital listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings)
- **Original digital pack on itch.io:** itch.io's current terms allow a publisher under 18 with legal parental/guardian consent. Its Creator FAQ says creator pages are free, revenue share is configurable, hosted downloads and analytics are included, and books/tools are supported. It has a smaller audience fit for Python worksheets than Etsy, but avoids a listing fee. [itch.io terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) [itch.io Creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq.amp)
- **PlaytestCloud:** a conditional micro-income route. Its current tester pages say children up to age 15 can participate through a parent profile with supervision and consent verification. A typical 15-minute playtest plus survey is advertised at USD 5-9, but onboarding is unpaid, invites are location-dependent, and the Netherlands is still part of a lower-availability global panel. It records screen/audio during tests, so guardian privacy review is mandatory. [PlaytestCloud child profile](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/398-how-to-add-your-kids-to-your-profile) [Rewards](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/118-how-much-money-will-i-earn) [Privacy](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/380-keeping-your-data-safe-online)
- **Fiverr Python help:** a guardian-managed marketplace test. Fiverr currently permits ages 13-17 only through a parent- or legal-guardian-owned account with permission, with the minor's involvement disclosed in the profile and gig. The prepared gig offers narrowly scoped beginner explanations/debugging, not schoolwork, passwords, remote access, or unsafe code. [Fiverr minor policy](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32567580782609-Navigating-Fiverr-as-a-minor-How-to-stay-safe-and-compliant) [Draft](fiverr-python-gig-draft.md)
- **Paid university youth research:** a conditional gift-card route. Utrecht University's Luister! panel accepts some 12-18-year-olds and advertises travel reimbursement plus a EUR 25 gift card per meet-up; STARr2.0 advertises EUR 90 for questionnaires but asks sensitive mental-health questions and has a larger time commitment. Neither is guaranteed to be recruiting for every applicant, so guardian review and truthful eligibility are required. [Luister! panel](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/luister/wie-zijn-wij/jongeren-panel) [STARr2.0](https://www.uu.nl/onderzoek/starr2/jongeren-en-ouders/onderzoek-achtergrond-en-opzet) [Assessment](paid-youth-research-opportunities.md)

HackerOne's current policy says rewards/payments are available only to adults, and Bugcrowd requires at least 18 or the age of majority for monetary compensation. Use local labs and explicit authorized programs for learning only; never test random systems. [HackerOne policy](https://www.hackerone.com/policies/privacy) [Bugcrowd terms](https://www.bugcrowd.com/resources/hacker-resources/standard-disclosure-terms/) [Assessment](bug-bounty-learning-route.md)

## Conditional local-service route

- **Babysits:** its current terms set a minimum babysitter age of 15 and require parent permission for anyone under 18. The platform says the babysitter chooses suitable families, recommends a public meeting before the first booking, and places tax, legal-obligation, and liability-insurance checks on the babysitter. Registration also involves personal details such as name, address, postcode, and bank account. Use only with a guardian, daytime/early-evening boundaries, a reachable adult as backup, and a family the guardian has screened. [Babysits terms](https://en.babysits.nl/terms/)
- **Petbnb:** not a current route at age 15. Its terms allow minors to use the service only with legal-representative permission, but require users to be at least 18 to enter a sitting agreement. [Petbnb terms](https://www.petbnb.nl/en/algemene-voorwaarden)

## Survey checkpoint

- **Toluna and LifePoints:** currently 16+ in their membership rules. LifePoints says surveys commonly take 10-20 minutes and pay through points, gift cards, or PayPal credit, but the panel can process detailed personal and sometimes sensitive data.
- **Prolific, UserTesting, and Upwork:** 18+.
- **AttaPoll:** its Dutch site is live and its terms say 13+; anyone under 18 must review the terms with a parent or guardian and obtain consent. Its privacy policy says it collects survey responses, location, device information, and may share data with survey partners and payment providers. Treat this as an optional low-value route only after a guardian reviews the privacy and payout terms; do not install the app or create an account automatically.
- **Microsoft Rewards:** Microsoft says users under 13 (or the applicable child age) need guardian consent. In the Netherlands this is mainly points and Microsoft/Xbox rewards rather than cash, so it is a low-priority side route.

At the assumed age of 15, do not sign up for a survey service without guardian review and explicit approval. Recheck after the next birthday or if the age assumption changes; never enter a false date of birth.

## Application gate

Do not click the application button or submit personal details until Daniel confirms the location and availability and a parent or guardian is present/approves the application. Do not claim availability, experience, or age details that have not been verified.

## Legal check

For a 15-year-old in the Netherlands, keep work light and non-industrial, avoid cashier work, machines, dangerous substances, heavy lifting, bicycle delivery, and alcohol-service areas. During school weeks, the general limit is 2 hours per school day and 12 hours per school week. Check the current government rules before accepting shifts.
