# Sell existing items checklist

Use this only for items Daniel already owns and is allowed to sell. The goal is quick, honest cash without buying inventory or taking resale risk.

## Guardian gate

- [ ] Guardian chooses the account and controls all messages, payments, shipping, identity checks, and handovers.
- [ ] If using Vinted, the account is in the guardian's name and no personal data of the minor is uploaded.
- [ ] Guardian approves every item, price, photo, and listing text before publishing.
- [ ] No counterfeit, stolen, locked, unsafe, or ownership-unclear items.
- [ ] No buyer meets Daniel alone; use a public handover with the guardian present.

## Listing rules

- Use clear photos on a plain background; remove school, address, faces, documents, and serial numbers unless the guardian intentionally approves disclosure.
- Describe condition, missing parts, and defects honestly.
- Check comparable prices and set a minimum before publishing.
- Keep the conversation and payment in the platform's approved flow where available.
- Save a copy of the listing and final agreement until the sale is complete.
- Record the date, sale price, platform fees, shipping cost, and net payout for the guardian's records.

## Stop immediately if

- A buyer sends a payment screenshot instead of confirmed payment.
- A buyer asks for a password, bank login, verification code, gift card, crypto deposit, or payment outside the agreed safe method.
- A buyer offers much more than the asking price, insists on foreign shipping, or pressures Daniel to act without the guardian.
