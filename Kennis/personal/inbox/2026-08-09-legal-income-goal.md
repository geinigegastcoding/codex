# Legal income goal

## Objective

Find and pursue legal ways to earn money without spending money, damaging the computer, impersonating Daniel, spamming people, or taking high-risk financial actions.

## Working assumptions

- The personal profile says Daniel is 15 and likely in the Netherlands. Verify if this is outdated before submitting anything.
- A parent or legal guardian must supervise account-based selling, contracts, payments, and tax handling where required.

## Priority routes

1. **Fast cash:** light, non-industrial paid work that is legal for a 15-year-old, such as supermarket shelf work, suitable shop or recreation work, or other permitted local work.
2. **Local service work:** babysitting is a conditional option because Babysits allows registration from age 15 with parent permission, but it carries safety, privacy, tax, and insurance responsibilities.
3. **Higher-upside skill work:** a small website, Python, or AI-workflow service delivered directly with guardian oversight. Start with one clearly scoped result, not a large package.
4. **Filler income:** legitimate survey panels only when age and country rules allow them. Treat this as low-value spare-time work, not the main plan.

## Excluded or restricted routes

- Prolific, UserTesting, and Upwork require participants/freelancers to be 18+.
- Fiverr and Etsy can be used by a minor only through a parent/guardian-owned account under their rules.
- Crypto trading is not an income plan: it is speculative, can lose the full stake, creates tax/accounting obligations, and should not be funded as part of this goal.

## Operating rules

- Research, drafting, local preparation, and read-only checks are allowed.
- Creating accounts, submitting applications, sending messages, uploading personal data, accepting paid work, and moving money require confirmation immediately before the action; guardian involvement is required when applicable.
- Keep a simple record of opportunity, time spent, gross income, fees, and payout status.
- Do not open a shop, payment-service contract, or business account alone. KVK says minors need parent/guardian permission to act; handlichting is only available from age 16.

## Next actions

- Identify the target town/area and whether the age assumption is current.
- Prepare one short, guardian-supervised service listing and one job-application template.
- Keep the verified Python tutoring/mini-pack assets and the conditional Babysits profile ready for guardian review.
- Build a small shortlist of real, current opportunities and update it on the recurring check.
