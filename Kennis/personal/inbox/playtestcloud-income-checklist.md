# PlaytestCloud income route

This is a conditional micro-income route for a 15-year-old in the Netherlands. No profile, app, recording, or payout method has been created.

If the guardian approves after reviewing this page, the official starting point is [PlaytestCloud's playtester signup](https://app.playtestcloud.com/signup). The signup page says the qualification test is unpaid and requires an Android, iOS, or PC device, headphones, and quiet time. Do not open it with personal details until approval is explicit.

## What the current official pages say

- PlaytestCloud says kids' playtests are available up to age 15; a parent must add the child to the parent's profile, supervise the test, and verify consent. Kids may not create their own profile. [Add kids](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/398-how-to-add-your-kids-to-your-profile) [Kids playtests](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/144-playtesting-with-kids)
- A typical 15-minute playtest plus survey is usually advertised at USD 5-9, but onboarding is unpaid and invites depend on location, age, device, and game interests. [Rewards](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/118-how-much-money-will-i-earn) [Invites](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/101-new-game)
- Rewards are delivered through Tremendous and may be redeemed as local gift cards, virtual cards, donations, or PayPal. PlaytestCloud says payment can arrive within up to three days after a good completion. [Reward options](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/211-what-reward-options-are-there)
- The Netherlands is included in the global panel, but PlaytestCloud says global availability is still beta and invitations may be delayed. [Non-US/UK/Canada testers](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/138-can-i-become-a-playtester-if-i-m-not-from-the-usa-uk-or-canada)

## Guardian gate

- [ ] Guardian reads the current tester terms and privacy information.
- [ ] Guardian owns the profile/email and completes any identity or consent verification.
- [ ] If the guardian chooses the child-playtest route, the official profile flow asks for the child's first name, date of birth, gender, and favorite games; the guardian enters these directly and checks them for accuracy. Do not send these details through chat or put them in a public file.
- [ ] Guardian accepts that the app may record the whole screen, microphone, first name, age, country, and gaming interests for a test. Developers do not receive the tester's last name, email, or postal address according to the current privacy FAQ. [Data and recording](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/380-keeping-your-data-safe-online)
- [ ] Use only a clean device with notifications, personal email, passwords, private documents, and banking apps closed or hidden before recording.
- [ ] Never enter a real card, PayPal login, password, verification code, or purchase into a game. PlaytestCloud warns that live-game payment prompts can charge real money and are not reimbursed. [No-cost tester rule](https://players.playtestcloud.com/article/129-do-i-have-to-pay-to-be-a-playtester)
- [ ] Guardian approves the reward email and whether gift card, virtual card, donation, or PayPal is suitable.

## Expected economics

This is side income, not reliable employment. The onboarding is unpaid, invites are not guaranteed, and a test can be rejected if the recording, spoken commentary, tasks, or survey is incomplete. Use a simple log: invite date, expected reward, minutes, completed, payout date, payout type, and any fee.

## Stop conditions

Stop before joining if the flow asks for money, a false age, a personal bank login, unknown software outside the official app flow, or access to private files. Decline tests that require showing private messages, school records, personal accounts, or sensitive information on screen.
