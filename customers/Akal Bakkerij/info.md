## Best landing-page angle

**Positioning:** neighbourhood Turkish bakery and casual takeaway in Leiden: fresh bakery snacks, döner, lahmacun, wraps, kapsalon, boxes and desserts, available all day from 08:00 to 20:00.

**Useful proof points:** 4.6/5 from 284 Google reviews; takeaway + delivery; open every day; accessible entrance; menu starts at EUR1.00.

**CTA hierarchy:** Call `06 51346253` -> Get directions -> Order via Thuisbezorgd.

**Customer language from reviews:** friendly/welcoming staff, fresh and delicious food, fast service, cheese rings, kapsalon, döner kebab, lahmacun and baklava.
